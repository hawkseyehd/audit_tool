# Product Context

## Product

Website Audit Tool is a professional command-line audit assistant for web teams, consultants,
and agencies. It scans public business websites, preserves evidence, normalizes findings, and
produces reports that help a reviewer explain priorities to a client.

## Primary Users

- Audit practitioners who run and review website audits.
- Consultants and agencies who deliver findings to business clients.
- Web teams who need a repeatable first-pass quality assessment.

## Client Report Purpose

The client report must turn technical scan output into an accurate, calm, and actionable review.
It should make the overall condition, most important risks, evidence, and next steps easy to scan
without hiding detail or implying that automation replaces expert review.

## Positioning

- Precise rather than promotional.
- Trustworthy rather than dramatic.
- Business-readable without weakening technical evidence.
- Neutral and reusable across clients.

## Personality

Clear, measured, evidence-led, practical, and quietly polished.

## Visual Direction

- White paper surface with near-black text.
- Cobalt accents for structure and emphasis.
- Semantic red, amber, green, and blue reserved for status and severity.
- Compact information hierarchy suitable for repeated professional review.
- No agency branding, decorative gradients, oversized marketing typography, or ornamental art.

## Accessibility

- Strong text contrast and readable print sizing.
- Meaning is never communicated by color alone.
- Semantic document structure and descriptive labels.
- Logical reading order in HTML and exported PDF.
- Automated accessibility results include the required manual-review limitation.
